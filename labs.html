The labs

Each design in this project raised questions that only a lab can answer. So each version came with its own experiments, written up as run sheets a lab could follow. They are all here, one table for each version, in the order the versions were thought of. Open an experiment to read its run sheet. Most also have a codebook and a dry-run report, so you can see what a finished run would look like.

V1. The bioengineered organism The design · What a lab would need

No.ExperimentPaperwork

2 Do genes carry abilities?Normal C. elegans against a strain with the drying-protection gene switched off, under UV, freezing and Martian dust. Codebook Dry run

V3. Earth microbes, no plumbing The design · What a lab would need

No.ExperimentPaperwork
1 Does Azospira clean dirt, and not just water?Twelve jars of Martian regolith simulant, perchlorate, and hydrogen as the only fuel. If it fails, there is no stage 1. Codebook Dry run
2 How much perchlorate can an earthworm stand?Nobody has measured it. Eighteen boxes, six doses of perchlorate, and the number that says when the cleaning can stop. Codebook Dry run
3 Does Xanthobacter grow in Martian dirt?It grows on three gases in a flask. Twelve jars ask whether it does the same in regolith, and keeps fixing nitrogen there. Codebook Dry run
4 Does the added gene blow the chlorine off?The one genuinely new thing in the design. A salt marsh plant gene in a gas-fed bacterium, asked to turn chloride into a gas. Codebook Dry run

V4. The bioengineered plant The design · What a lab would need

No.ExperimentPaperwork
1 Is the plant poisoning itself?Sixteen pots, and one group is fed ammonium instead of nitrate. If those plants recover, the mechanism is right. Codebook Dry run
2 Does cleaning the pot rescue the plant?A live bacterium doing the job the gene would do, with no genetic modification anywhere. Codebook Dry run
3 Does root acid unlock Martian rock?The one job the simulation could not test. Mars simulant, a weak acid found in lemons, and a radish. Codebook Dry run
4 Where does the perchlorate end up?A crop that grows well and concentrates perchlorate in the part people eat is worse than no crop at all. Codebook Dry run

V5. A plant on open Mars The design · What a lab would need

No.ExperimentPaperwork
1 Does a cold leaf hold its water at Martian pressure?A vacuum chamber, a cold plate and some leaves. One afternoon, and it tests the claim the whole design rests on. Codebook Dry run
2 Can a plant dry out and revive, night after night?Trays of desert moss and a timer. The cheapest experiment in the project and the one most likely to end version 5. Codebook Dry run
