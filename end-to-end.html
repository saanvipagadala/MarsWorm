The whole project, end to end

Optional reading. Nothing here is being asked of you. It is six stages on one page, in the order they happened, with a way into each.

Where it stands. Stages 1 and 2 are finished. Stages 3 to 6 are designed, written up and waiting: the first two need a bench, the last two need an organism nobody has built yet.

The six stages

The stage The question What came of it
1 Searching Earth for MarsWorm Is there already something alive that could make soil on Mars? No. All 322 sequenced organisms that turned up were scored on 23 jobs. The best reached 14. The corner where one organism does both jobs is empty ✅
2 Designing MarsWorm If nothing has all 23 abilities, can they be borrowed? 37 genes from 18 organisms, 41,445 letters of DNA. Every one real, published, and already doing that job somewhere ✅
3 Testing Biology Enriches Dirt Does adding life to dead dirt actually turn it into soil? The assumption the whole project rests on, and nobody has checked it. 15 cups, five recipes, earthworms in some ⏳
4 Testing Genes Enable Survival Does a gene bring its ability with it? Tested backwards: break the one gene behind an ability in a living worm, and see whether the ability goes. 48 plates ⏳
5 Building MarsWorm How do 37 genes become one living thing? Into Azotobacter vinelandii, which already carries 13 of them. The other 24 go in over six rounds, each tested before the next is ordered ✅
6 Testing MarsWorm in Dirt Does MarsWorm actually turn Martian dirt into soil? 18 cups, six recipes. One of them is the point: Martian dirt and MarsWorm, nothing added ⏳

✅ finished · ⏳ written up, waiting for a bench or an organism

The two that carry the most weight

Stage 6, group 5. Every other way of enriching dirt starts by adding something that was once alive: compost, leaves, scraps. Mars has none of that. An organism that only works when it is fed has not solved the problem, so the one cup that matters holds simulant and MarsWorm and nothing else.

Stage 5, round 3. Nobody has ever made A. vinelandii breathe perchlorate, and the genes that clear that poison give off oxygen, which wrecks the fertiliser machine the same microbe was chosen for. It is a genuine bet, and it is reached early on purpose, before anything expensive depends on it.

What would make this wrong

If this fails Then
Stage 3 Adding life to regolith does not make soil, and the whole target was wrong
Stage 4 Abilities do not travel with genes, and a 37-gene shopping list means much less than it looks like
Round 3 of Stage 5 A. vinelandii cannot cope with perchlorate, and the design has nowhere else to go

Each of those is a real possibility, and each has an experiment or a round whose job is to find it out early.
