The short version

MarsWorm did not beat what was already there. On this evidence the engineering has not yet earned its place over an ordinary earthworm.

How each answer was worked out

Which two recipes each question actually put head to head. Every one was decided before the experiment started, not gone looking for afterwards because the answer looked good.

How a winner is decided: “the worst cup of one group still beat the best cup of the other”. If even the unluckiest cup on one side did better than the luckiest cup on the other, the recipe won, and you can check that by looking at the chart. The gap also has to reach 0.10 g, about one seedling, because smaller than that is too close to call.
