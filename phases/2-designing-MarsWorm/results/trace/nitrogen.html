1. What this trait needs done

A trait is rarely one thing. Each job below needs its own protein.

Job What we search for Why it is a separate job
Pull plant food out of thin air nitrogenase plants cannot use the nitrogen in air and this is the only enzyme that converts it. Nitrogenase is a machine, not one protein: it needs its partner chains and a whole crew to build the metal cluster at its heart. Those parts are not listed here – they are discovered from what UniProt says about the enzyme itself. It also needs an oxygen-protected pocket, a real engineering constraint rather than a reason to skip the job
