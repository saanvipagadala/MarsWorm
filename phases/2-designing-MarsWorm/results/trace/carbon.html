Make food out of thin air

How this trait became 2 genes to order.

All 37 genes · How the search works · Every trait

1. What this trait needs done

A trait is rarely one thing. Each job below needs its own protein.

Job What we search for Why it is a separate job
Catch carbon dioxide straight out of the air ribulose-bisphosphate carboxylase Mars air is 95% carbon dioxide – the most abundant thing on the planet. Every other enrichment job eats carbon that is already there; this is the only one that MAKES it, and without it the soil can only recycle what was shipped from Earth
Keep the carbon-catching cycle turning phosphoribulokinase the catcher grabs carbon dioxide onto a partner molecule and is then stuck until that partner is rebuilt. This rebuilds it, so the first enzyme can go round again

Term source: UniProt KW-0120 Carbon dioxide fixation

Term source: the Calvin cycle

4. How the winner is picked

From the survivors, one protein per job. Ties break in this order:

  1. nothing rules it out
  2. the term it matched sits higher in the job’s list
  3. not an over-studied lab organism
  4. better curation score
  5. shorter, because you pay per letter of DNA
