How sure can we be, with three cups? Every comparison above asks one thing: did the two groups overlap? If the worst cup in one group still beat the best cup in the other, that is as strong as three cups can ever get. If the ranges cross, the honest answer is that we could not tell them apart and not that they are the same.

A difference also has to be big enough to matter. A result here needs a gap of at least 10% of the whole scale, bare regolith to real potting soil, which is 0.635 g, so at least 0.064 g. Without that rule, plants could triple in weight, still be dead, and get called a success.
