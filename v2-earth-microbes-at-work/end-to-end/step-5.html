Step 5. The growing bed, where regolith becomes soil

Everything before this made ingredients: washed regolith, unlocked phosphorus and iron, and bacteria full of nitrogen. None of it is soil yet. In the growing bed, earthworms and the microbes they carry mix it, rot it, and turn it into soil a plant can grow in.

Why the bed is needed

  • Clean regolith is not soil. By now the regolith has been washed of perchlorate in step 1 and its phosphorus and iron unlocked in step 2. It is clean and it is fed. But nothing is alive in it, and two things are still missing.
  • Soil needs organic matter. Organic matter is the dead and living remains woven through soil. It is what makes soil crumble in your hand and hold water. Mars regolith has never held any. The Rhodobacter and Azospira cells arriving from steps 3 and 4, and the Bacillus still living in the regolith from step 2, are the first organic matter it has ever had.
  • And soil needs something to break that organic matter down. The nitrogen a plant needs is locked inside those bacteria as protein. On Earth a whole crowd of soil creatures, worms, mites, fungi and more, breaks it out. Mars has none of them. So we bring earthworms, and each earthworm brings a gut full of microbes with it.

Why the first bed is mostly starter compost

  • Earthworms cannot live in pure Mars regolith. It is too harsh for them, and they do not last in it. So the first bed is about three quarters starter compost, which they are used to, and a quarter regolith.
  • Each bed after that takes more regolith. The habitat makes its own compost over time, from food scraps and the crew’s waste, and from the soil the first beds made. So each new bed can lean less on Earth, until one day a bed is nearly all Mars.

Why it matters

  • Steps 1 to 4 make ingredients, not soil. Washed regolith, unlocked phosphorus and iron, bacteria full of nitrogen, and clean water. Mixed together, that is still dirt with things in it.
  • Step 5 is where it becomes soil. The earthworms and their microbes do most of that work, and they are the only living things in the whole design that make soil rather than ingredients for it.
