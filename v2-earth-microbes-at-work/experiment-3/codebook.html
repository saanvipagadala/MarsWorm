Codebook · V2 · Exp 3 · Does an earthworm free phosphorus from Martian regolith?

V2 is Earth microbes at work, and this is one of its three experiments. Every sheet, every column.

Four spreadsheets hold this experiment, printed and filled in by hand at the bench, then typed up exactly as written. This page says what belongs in every column. Write down only what an instrument gives you: a weight, the reading from the phosphorus test, a count of seedlings.

The twelve boxes

Four treatments, three boxes each.

Treatment What is in it Why it is here Boxes
A simulant and compost, no worms the control P01 to P03
B simulant and compost, with worms the question: B against A P04 to P06
C simulant and compost, with castings, no live worm the live worm against what it leaves behind P07 to P09
D compost only, with worms the background: worms with no rock present P10 to P12

Set the boxes out in the shuffled bench_position order from boxes.csv, never grouped by treatment, and label them by number only. The key stays with this sheet.

The four sheets

boxes.csv

The register. Filled in once on day one and never changed.

Download boxes.csv · blank, ready to fill in

Column What goes in it
box_id P01 to P12, written on the box in marker
treatment A to D
treatment_name the recipe
bench_position 1 to 12, left to right on the shelf, in the shuffled order
start_mass_g the mass of bedding that went in, to 0.1 g
worms_in_g the weight of the worms that went in, to 0.01 g. 0 for A and C
date_started YYYY-MM-DD

phosphorus.csv

The test, twice: on day zero, before anything has happened, and at week eight. Without the day-zero line the experiment has nothing to compare against. Every week-eight sample is run in one session, by one method.

Download phosphorus.csv · blank, ready to fill in

Column What goes in it
box_id which box
day 0 or 56
date YYYY-MM-DD
sample_dry_g the weight of the dried sample that was tested
available_p_mg_per_kg the reading from the kit or the spectrophotometer, as it comes
method which kit or instrument, the same for every box
notes anything about the sample

radish.csv

The measure that counts. A test kit says what the chemistry sees. A radish says what a root can actually get.

Download radish.csv · blank, ready to fill in

Column What goes in it
box_id which box
date_sown YYYY-MM-DD
date_harvested YYYY-MM-DD, day 21 after sowing
seedlings_kept how many were left after thinning, normally 3
dry_plant_weight_g all the seedlings from the box, dried and weighed together, to 0.01 g. This is the answer
notes nothing to record

care_log.csv

The weekly dampness check through the eight quiet weeks.

Download care_log.csv · blank, ready to fill in

Column What goes in it
box_id which box
day 7, 14, … 56
date YYYY-MM-DD
water_added_ml distilled water added, if any
notes anything unusual: a dry box, a disturbed box, a smell

A blank cell is not a zero

A blank means not measured. A 0 means measured, and it was zero. Those are different facts. If something was not measured, leave the cell empty.
