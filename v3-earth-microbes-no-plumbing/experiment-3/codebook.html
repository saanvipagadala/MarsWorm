Experiment 3: the codebook, every sheet, every column

Four spreadsheets hold this experiment, printed and filled in by hand at the bench, then typed up exactly as written. This page says what belongs in every column. Write down what the meter showed. Growth rates belong in the report, not on the sheet.

The twelve jars

Four treatments, three jars each.

Level What is in it Jars What it answers
A damp simulant, gas mix, bacterium X01 to X03 the design
B standard medium, gas mix, bacterium X04 to X06 the published result, and the yardstick
C simulant, gas mix, bacterium, fertiliser X07 to X09 what the nitrogen fixing is worth
D simulant, gas mix, no bacterium X10 to X12 what the dirt reads on its own

Set the jars out in the shuffled bench_position order from jars.csv, never grouped by treatment, and label them by number only.

The gas is roughly 70 parts hydrogen, 15 nitrogen, 10 carbon dioxide and 5 oxygen. The oxygen is deliberately low, because the enzyme that catches nitrogen is wrecked by it.

The four sheets

growth.csv

Twice a week for five weeks. This is the sheet that answers the question.

Download growth.csv · blank, ready to fill in

Column What goes in it
jar_id which jar
day 0, 3, 7, 10, 14, 17, 21, 24, 28, 31 or 35
date YYYY-MM-DD
od600 what the meter read, before subtracting anything. The dirt reads above zero, and the sterile jars are what that is subtracted with
ph regolith drifts alkaline, and that alone can stop growth
gas_reflushed y or n. A jar that ran out of gas stopped being the experiment
notes a cloudy jar, a grain in the cuvette, a repeated reading

The sheets with invented numbers in them

The same four sheets also exist filled in by a script. This set is the expected outcome: it grows in regolith, at about a third of what it manages in medium, and the fertiliser buys only a little.

jars · growth · nitrogen · care log · a dry run, not a result

A blank cell is not a zero

A blank means not measured. A 0 means measured, and there was none. If a jar was not read that day, leave the cell empty.
