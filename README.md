## What it is about

A Mars crew cannot carry enough food, so a colony has to grow its own, and
growing food needs soil. Mars has none. It has regolith: crushed rock with
nothing dead in it, nothing alive in it, and perchlorate salts that poison
what you plant.

On Earth that job belongs to earthworms, or really to the microbes in their
guts. An earthworm on Mars dies within minutes. So the project asks whether
one organism could be designed to do the worm's job **and** survive Mars, and
then writes down the experiments that would prove or break the answer.

Designing that organism came to 37 genes. Then the design was tested against
published metabolic models, honestly, and most of it did not survive. What came
out instead is [a way of making soil](https://saanvipagadala.github.io/MarsWorm/v2-earth-microbes-at-work/end-to-end.html)
that uses **three ordinary Earth microbes, with no genetic
modification at all**. The engineered organism is now
[one of three tracks](https://saanvipagadala.github.io/MarsWorm/v2-earth-microbes-at-work/index.html#three-tracks)
rather than the only one.

waiting on a bench. Stages 5 and 6 are the plan for the engineered organism,
which stage 7 showed is not the only way to get soil.
